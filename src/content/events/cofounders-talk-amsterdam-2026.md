Co-founders Chen Alon and Sulaiman Khatib, with activist Iris Gur, spoke about
their nonviolent struggle against the occupation at a public event in Amsterdam,
hosted by Joden Zeggen Nee and facilitated by Sandra Rottenberg. During their
visit they also met members of the local support group and spoke with Dutch
media about the movement's work.
