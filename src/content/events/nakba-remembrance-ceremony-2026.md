A screening of the 7th Joint Nakba Remembrance Ceremony, hosted by Combatants
for Peace, held on Friday 15 May 2026. The evening began at 18:00 with a shared
community dinner at Buurcentrum de Horizon, followed by a live broadcast of the
ceremony from Beit Jala and personal stories carrying the weight of memory,
resilience and the call for truth and justice for the Palestinian people.
