A live screening of the 21st Israeli-Palestinian Joint Memorial Ceremony,
held on 20 April 2026 at 19:15 in Café Belcampo, Amsterdam. The ceremony —
the largest jointly organised Israeli-Palestinian peace event — is broadcast
live from Israel-Palestine and watched by tens of thousands as a shared act of
remembrance. The screening was followed by a moderated discussion for attendees
to reflect and share together.
